Welcome to Evara

Not just a stay,
a Himalayan abode.

Evara Woodhouse is a boutique mountain stay in Shangarh, Himachal Pradesh. Come for the meadows, stay for the mountain air, warm wooden spaces and a slower rhythm of life.

Thoughtfully placed in nature, it is a welcoming base for exploring the beauty of Sainj Valley and the Great Himalayan National Park region.
