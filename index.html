Guest Information

When you enquire, please share your travel dates, number of guests and room preference. Room availability and details are confirmed directly by the property.

Inside The Property

Wooden rooms with big windows and running balconies, a sunny courtyard, a social balcony on the first floor and a quiet lounge on the second floor. Wi-Fi, parking, hot water, housekeeping and laundry are available. Room heater is available on request at an additional charge.

Road, Snow & Weather

Mountain weather and road conditions can change quickly. Please check with us before you travel, especially in winter and during the monsoon.

Care & Responsibility

Please help us look after the wooden property, the meadow and the surroundings, and respect the quiet of other guests and the valley.
