About Evara

Not just a stay,
a Himalayan abode.

Evara Woodhouse is a boutique mountain stay in Shangarh, Himachal Pradesh. Come for the meadows, stay for the mountain air, warm wooden spaces and a slower rhythm of life.

Thoughtfully placed in nature, it is a welcoming base for exploring the beauty of Sainj Valley and the Great Himalayan National Park region.

Guest Information

Facilities at the Property

Wi-Fi, parking, housekeeping and laundry are available. Every room has a geyser. Room heaters are available on request at an additional charge.

Road, Snow & Weather

Mountain weather and road conditions can change. Please check local conditions before travelling and allow extra time for the journey.

Bonfire & Transport

Bonfire evenings are available on request. Ask the property team about transport or pick-up support from Sainj or Aut.
